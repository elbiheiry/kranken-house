Surgical Residency Training Management System
MVP Scope &amp; Technical Work Plan
Project Overview
A web-based platform designed to manage and monitor surgical residency training through digital operation logging, approval workflows, progress tracking, and competency-based reporting.
The system aims to improve transparency in operative exposure and simplify training management within surgical residency programs.
Project Objectives
Digitize surgical training records
Track operative experience for residents
Provide structured approval workflows
Monitor training progress against requirements
Support training directors with centralized dashboards
Improve fairness and visibility in case distribution
